How your house gets built

Six clear steps, from your first call to the day you move in.

  1. Enquiry and site discussion

    Tell us about your plot, budget and needs. We discuss your ideas and answer your questions.

  2. 2D plan and 3D design

    We draw the layout and show the exterior in 3D. You review and we revise until you are happy.

  3. Estimate and agreement

    You receive a clear quote based on your package. Specifications and schedule go into a written agreement.

  4. Foundation and structure

    Construction begins with the foundation and structure, with quality checks at each stage.

  5. Finishing work

    Plastering, flooring, fittings, painting and all finishing as agreed for your package.

  6. Handover

    We do a final walkthrough with you and hand over your home.

Frequently asked questions

Our packages are Super at ₹2,350/sq.ft, Deluxe at ₹2,450/sq.ft and Premium at ₹2,650/sq.ft. The exact specification for each package is written into your agreement.

Do you build outside Chennai?

Yes. We build in Chennai, Tiruvannamalai and across Tamil Nadu.
